# Investor discipline and assumption review

Apply these checks before the final recommendation. These are adaptations of published methods, not a consensus formula or a promise of elite returns. Forecast validation means testing plausibility and consistency, never certifying the future.

## Business economics before price targets

- State how the company earns cash, why customers choose it, and what could destroy that advantage. If a material segment cannot be understood or valued, identify the exposure and withhold conclusions that depend on it. A polished narrative is not a circle of competence.
- Support a claimed moat with observable evidence such as retention, pricing after volume effects, cost position or distribution economics, and examine rival evidence. High ROE/ROCE alone may reflect leverage, accounting or a favorable cycle. Separate structural advantage from current profitability.
- Assess incremental reinvestment economics, using multi-year changes with acquisition, cycle and accounting caveats. Avoid unstable incremental-return ratios with tiny/negative denominators. Test growth funding, working capital, capacity utilization and dilution; growth that consumes capital without sufficient returns need not create value.
- Limited internal growth is not an automatic rejection: evaluate cash returned or redeployed, acquisitions, debt repayment and buybacks against per-share value. Check management promises against delivery. EPS accretion alone does not establish a good acquisition or buyback.
- For nonfinancial businesses, reconcile owner earnings when useful: attributable earnings plus justified noncash adjustments, less maintenance investment and additional working capital needed to sustain operations. Maintenance capex is often uncertain; show a range and basis, never assume it equals depreciation. Starting from CFO already includes working-capital effects; avoid deducting them twice. Treat stock compensation and dilution as economic costs without double counting. Keep owner earnings, CFO-minus-capex and FCFF distinct. Use equity/capital analysis for financial firms.

## Price, expectations and survival

- Distinguish an excellent business from an attractive stock at the observed price. Explain the earnings, margins, capital needs and valuation multiple necessary to earn the stated hurdle. Where feasible solve a reverse valuation and compare required performance with history, peers and capacity. Label it model-implied, not observed market consensus.
- Margin of safety against present fair value is `(present fair value − price) / present fair value`, for positive fair value. It differs from upside `(fair value − price) / price` and from future holding-period return. State the valuation uncertainty and why the chosen entry range compensates for it. Never attribute a universal 20%/30% rule to Buffett.
- Stress permanent capital loss: refinancing failure, covenant/liquidity pressure, dilution, impaired economics and governance. Inspect debt maturities, restricted cash and contingent obligations. A low multiple cannot compensate mechanically for an unquantifiable material risk.
- In one-year mode, preserve the deadline and test no rerating, delayed execution and trading liquidity. Separate earnings/dividend return from multiple expansion. Long-term patience is not evidence that a discount closes before a mandatory sale. Do not extend the deadline to rescue the thesis.

## Required assumption challenge

Include a compact assumption table in the saved report. Cover every decision-driving forecast, including growth, margins/credit costs, reinvestment, dilution, dividends, exit multiples and discount rates where applicable:

| Driver and horizon | Fact/guidance/analyst assumption | Source IDs and historical/peer basis | Bear/base/bull range | Counterevidence and dependencies | Sensitivity or action-changing level | Review indicator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use null/unavailable for missing evidence, not an invented baseline. Check consistency between growth and funding, capex and capacity, dividends and liquidity, and terminal growth and reinvestment. Explain sector/period mismatches. Concentrate sensitivity work on assumptions that change the decision; jointly stress related drivers when one-at-a-time shocks would hide risk.

Then state the strongest evidence-backed objection to the proposed action, revisit its original source and arithmetic, and record resolved, unresolved or thesis-breaking status with the effect on the action. A bull/bear role prompt is a checklist, not independent verification; agreement between model passes adds no source authority. Do not invent bearish facts or a mandatory downside percentage. Missing essential support prevents an unconditional Buy and requires withholding other dependent conclusions.

On subsequent runs compare recorded assumptions with actual comparable results. Explain errors in inputs, business forecasts, valuation assumptions and timing separately. Preserve old predictions; a profitable outcome does not prove sound reasoning, nor does a loss alone disprove it. Do not claim a validated strategy from a handful of reports.

## Original sources and boundaries

Reviewed 2026-10-05. These historical writings establish attribution, not current stock evidence or universal empirical proof.

- [Buffett, 1986 letter, owner-earnings appendix](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html): maintenance investment and estimation limits.
- [Buffett, 1992 letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1992.html): incremental capital economics, valuation and margin of safety.
- [Buffett, 1996 letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1996.html): understandability and durable earning power over long horizons.
- [Buffett, 2007 letter, business discussion](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2007ltr.pdf): competitive durability and differing capital requirements; low-growth cash generators can be valuable.
- [Howard Marks, I Beg to Differ (2022)](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo/i-beg-to-differ): expectations and the difficulty of being both different and right.
- [Howard Marks, The Folly of Certainty (2024)](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo/the-folly-of-certainty): uncertainty and outcome-versus-judgment discipline.
- [Fundsmith, 2024 annual letter](https://www.fundsmith.co.uk/media/pirmvyly/annual-letter-to-shareholders-2024.pdf): returns on capital, reinvestment and cash generation. Its FCF/net-income cash conversion differs from this skill’s CFO/PAT diagnostic; always label the definition.

Reddit discussions can suggest failure modes to test, not verify financial facts, author credentials or investment performance. Distinguish original posts/comments from generated related answers, ads and promotional links. Investor writings likewise require adaptation to Indian disclosure, governance and sector conditions; they do not replace NSE/BSE filings.
